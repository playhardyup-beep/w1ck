About

Kid Kwesi is an Afrotrap artist from Accra, Ghana. A childhood moment — a rap record playing in his brother's room — lit a spark that never went out. From writing his first rhymes as a kid to recording seriously from 2018, music has always been the constant.

Raised in Accra with big dreams and an even bigger appetite for more, Kid Kwesi found in music both a refuge and a purpose. His sound is rooted in truth, ambition, and the kind of hunger that only comes from simply wanting more.

The goal is simple — to be one of the biggest artists out of Africa. Every record is a step toward that.
